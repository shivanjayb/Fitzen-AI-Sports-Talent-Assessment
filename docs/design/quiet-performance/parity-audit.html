What was tested, and what remains unverified

Opened the seven main sections, Profile, Integrations, three onboarding steps, metric groups, AI settings, portrait studio and privacy. Exercised dashboard reorder/cancel, 7/30/90-day metric views, history selection, metric navigation, refresh and Escape dismissal.

No credentials, provider permissions, paid AI actions, uploads, new images, personal-file access, deletion, sign-out or export downloads. No reference preferences or health records saved. Connected, populated, error and authentication outcomes remain unverified. This audit updates reports only.

Correction: Dashboard editing loaded asynchronously. Reorder controls do work; the earlier “no visible change” observation was premature. Remove and final Done/persistence were not exercised.

Required before another completeness claim
  1. Restore the full Home inventory, editing, weekly mood and week-over-week summary before adding Fitzen tools.
  2. Match the shared metric explorer, all Vitals/Sleep signals, 7/30/90-day history, baseline, coverage and provenance.
