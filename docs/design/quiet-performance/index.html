Prepare once.
Take your time.

  1. Set your camera

    At hip height, around 2–3 metres away. Keep your whole body visible.

  2. Use the standard setup

    Personal ranges apply to free training. Guided tests use their own standard protocol.

  3. Rest when you need to

    Stop for pain, dizziness or illness. This is local screening, not an official SAI assessment.
